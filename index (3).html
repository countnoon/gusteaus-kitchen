ORDER G1GRAMMAR · discover12 min14 points

Spot the passive

In a kitchen, the food is more important than the person. So chefs often say what is done, not who does it. Find out how this works.

Part A find · 5 points

What to do: read the text. Seven verbs are underlined. Tap the five verbs where we do not know who does the action. Every wrong tap costs one point.

Part B the rule · 3 points

Active: The chefs cut the vegetables. (Who? The chefs.)

Passive: The vegetables are cut. (Who? Not important.)

Present passive = am / is / are + past participle (washed, cut, made)

Past passive = was / were + past participle

To say who does it, add by: The soup is made by Remy.

What to do: read the rule box. Then answer three questions about it.

Part C active or passive? · 6 points

What to do: read each sentence. Is it active or passive? Tap your answer.

How the materials meet the project criteria.

PrincipleHow it appears
Materials developmentOne theme (Ratatouille and Gusteau's restaurant), one grammar focus (passive voice), one learner profile (A2+, ages 14–16) across tasks L1–L3 and G1–G3.
AdaptationA real film scene is adapted with main-idea questions, an ordering task and true or false. A real recipe is rewritten as a short radio text and as a gap-fill recipe card.
Comprehensible inputKey words are taught before listening (L1). The video is watched twice. The audio has a slow speed and a “repeat sentence” button. A model text comes before the rule.
ScaffoldingA “What to do” box in every part, a rule box with examples, a word bank, and feedback with a short explanation after every answer. Learners can try again.
DifferentiationThree levels in G2: Commis Chef, Sous Chef, Head Chef. Three listening routes in L3: normal speed, slow speed, listening with the text. The best score is saved.
InclusionEasy-to-read font, large buttons, arrows instead of drag and drop, text for the audio, no time limits. Right and wrong answers are shown with ✓ and ✗, not only with colour. Works on a phone.
TechnologyInteractive web page with automatic checking, YouTube film clips, an audio player with browser text-to-speech, a team game for the projector.
EvaluationPoints for every task, a Chef's card with ranks, self-check with instant feedback, peer check in the team game, rubric below.
